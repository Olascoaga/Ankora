# UI principles

- Keep the molecular viewer central and visual-first.
- Present the workflow on the left, contextual inspection on the right, and status, warnings, provenance, commands, and logs below.
- Make consequential actions explicit and show evidence before transformation.
- Use stable warning codes with clear human explanations and actionable recovery options.
- Never imply that unavailable or future scientific functionality is implemented.
- Keep technical details expandable rather than hidden.
